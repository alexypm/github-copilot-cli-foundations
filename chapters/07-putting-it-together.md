## What You Will Do

Run one complete workflow from idea to verified change.

In this chapter, you will combine context, planning, changes, and verification into one end-to-end workflow.

## Goal

By the end of this chapter, you should be able to guide Copilot through a small end-to-end task.

## Follow Along

1. Start with context.
2. Ask for an analysis or plan.
3. Make a small change.
4. Review or test the result.
5. Summarize the work for the group.

## How To Follow Along

- Keep one task in front of you at a time.
- Use the answer from one step to start the next step.
- Pause after each result so the group can catch up.
- End by saying what changed and what you would do next.
