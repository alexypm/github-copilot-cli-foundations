# AI News Reports

Weekly summaries of notable AI industry news, gathered and written for
quick reference. Each report covers one week and includes a clear date
range plus source links for every item.

## Naming convention

Reports are named `YYYY-MM-DD-ai-news.md`, where the date is the last day
of the covered week (report generation date). Add new reports to the top
of the index below.
